Policies & controls

Foundational controls are in place; implementation evidence is still maturing.

Interim standard

Financial management & internal control

Requires organizational-purpose spending, documented approvals, transaction support, separate identification of restricted funds, periodic reconciliation, expense records and donor-reporting discipline. Separation of request, approval, payment and reconciliation is required where practical for YSI's size.

Adopted working policy

Safeguarding & code of conduct

Sets prevention, PSEA, child protection, dignity, non-retaliation, responsible communications and reporting standards. The policy is in force; a dedicated safeguarding focal point and additional escalation mechanisms are still to be appointed or strengthened.

Interim standard

Health, safety & environmental operating safeguards

Requires risk assessment, stop-work authority, PPE, waste-handling controls, hazardous-material isolation, coastal/water safety, incident reporting, first aid and field records. These controls form YSI's current practical environmental/social risk foundation.

Field cleanup provides access to real waste streams and operational constraints. YSI's institutional objective is to measure and characterize those streams, test realistic recovery pathways, validate environmental and economic performance, and replicate only what the evidence supports.

Monitoring & evidence

How YSI treats the 20,000+ kg figure.

Evidence reconstruction in progress

YSI's historical 20,000+ kg of waste removed figure is its stated public impact claim. Extensive field records document sustained cleanup, collection, transport, sorting and disposal activity at Lumley Beach, but the original mass-calculation source has not yet been recovered and the figure has not been independently verified.

Future monitoring standard: pilot operations will use auditable weight and mass-balance records that capture date, site, gross/net weight, weighing method, material category, destination, recovery pathway, responsible staff and supporting evidence. Public claims will be strengthened only when the underlying record supports them.
