Financial accountability

Build the control environment before claiming grant readiness.

Institutional banking verification in progress

YSI is establishing and verifying organizational banking, approved signatory controls, transaction support, reconciliations and restricted-fund tracking. Sensitive account details are not published. Banking verification will be made available to qualified institutional counterparts when the setup is fully evidenced.

Funding history: YSI is a newly incorporated organization and does not present itself as having already administered a major institutional grant. Earlier founder experience is kept separate from YSI's own institutional funding history.

Policies & safeguards

Public summaries backed by real working controls.

Financial management & internal control

Organizational-purpose spending, documented approvals, transaction support, restricted-fund identification, reconciliation, expense records and donor-reporting discipline.

Safeguarding & code of conduct

Prevention, PSEA, child protection, dignity, non-retaliation, responsible communications and reporting standards.

Health, safety & environmental safeguards

Risk assessment, stop-work authority, PPE, waste-handling controls, hazardous-material isolation, coastal/water safety, incident reporting and first aid.

Due-diligence principle

Website summaries do not substitute for underlying records. Qualified counterparties may request the relevant adopted policies and supporting institutional documents.
