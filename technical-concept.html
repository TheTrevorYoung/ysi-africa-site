Waste Project · Advanced technical concept library

Possible longer-term architecture — not the current project.

YSI Africa preserves this 16-step concept as a technical thinking library. The current Waste Project stage is Phase 0 — Evidence & Operational Design. Phase 0 is non-thermal and is designed to determine what the waste stream, operating environment, economics, safeguards and regulatory requirements actually justify.

Conditional concepts only. This sequence does not represent equipment or facilities currently owned or operated by YSI Africa. Steps 8–14 are conditional engineering hypotheses. Any later technical pathway would require characterization, engineering, economics, emissions and environmental safeguards, regulatory approval and a legitimate use/offtake case.

Conditional concept sequence

Sixteen steps retained for technical reference.

The sequence is deliberately separated from the main Waste Project page so the public project proposition remains focused on Intercept → Measure → Sort → Recover → Route → Learn → Replicate.

01

Intercept

Potential local interception, subject to site evidence and approval.

02

Collect

Compliant collection and haulage through authorized operating arrangements.

03

Measure

Auditable weight, location and load records.

04

Inspect

Hazard and contamination screening.

05

Sort

Material identification and characterization.

06

Separate

Separate streams for evidence-based routing.

07

Route

Recovery, treatment or disposal only through justified pathways.

08

Prepare Energy Stream

Long-term hypothesis only if characterization justifies it.

09

Dry

Conditional technical step, not Phase 0.

10

Shred

Conditional technical step, not Phase 0.

11

Produce RDF

Technical hypothesis requiring fuel-quality and market validation.

12

Heat the Dryer

Conditional thermal hypothesis requiring full safeguards and approvals.

13

Capture Residues

Conditional residue-control concept.

14

Recover or Control Ash

Any ash pathway would require compliant testing and regulation.

15

Replicate the Unit

A later unit would be considered only after a first operating model is proven.

16

Connect the Network

Multiple units could form a wider Freetown network only after evidence supports replication.
