Identity

Clear institutional identity.

Young Sustainability Initiative (YSI Africa) is a legally incorporated Sierra Leonean environmental nonprofit organized as a Company Limited by Guarantee.

YSI is independent, community-driven and focused on practical environmental systems in Sierra Leone, with initial operational concentration in Freetown.

This full name is used deliberately to distinguish the organization from unrelated institutions and companies that also use the acronym “YSI.”

Documented field work

Lumley Beach cleanup and sanitation work in Freetown provide direct experience with mixed coastal waste, public-space cleanup, manual material handling and the practical realities of waste leakage.

This field experience is the evidence base for YSI's progression from cleanup toward measurement, characterization, recovery testing and stronger waste systems.

Development-stage wetland project

Aberdeen Creek protection, baseline and ecological recovery.

YSI is developing an evidence-led Aberdeen Creek programme. The current phase focuses on authority coordination, site evidence, GIS and boundary information, waste-leakage pathways, ecological diagnosis and technical review before physical intervention.

YSI has current communication with NPAA concerning Aberdeen Creek and an active technical exchange with Mangrove Action Project. These relationships are described according to their actual status and are not presented as project approval or formal partnership.

No Aberdeen restoration-area, planting, survival, biodiversity-recovery or project-funding claim is being made by YSI at this stage.
