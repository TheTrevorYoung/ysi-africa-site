How to read YSI claims

A simple public evidence framework.

The label matters because each type of statement proves something different.

01 · VERIFIED FACTSupported and publishableA fact YSI can responsibly state as established.
02 · DOCUMENTED EVIDENCERecorded source materialPhotos, correspondence, field records or other evidence supporting a statement.
03 · PROPOSAL / HYPOTHESISStill to be testedA design, technical pathway or project concept that has not yet become a result.
04 · UNKNOWNNot yet establishedWhere evidence is missing, YSI should say so rather than fill the gap with assumptions.
