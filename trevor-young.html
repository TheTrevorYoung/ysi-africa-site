Founder profile · documented public record

Trevor Young

Trevor Young is Founder & Managing Director of Young Sustainability Initiative (YSI Africa). His current work focuses on building practical environmental systems in Sierra Leone across waste management, sanitation, circular economy, coastal and wetland protection, and evidence-led project development.

Current focus

From environmental field work to practical systems.

YSI Africa is developing a localized waste-interception and circular resource-recovery model in Freetown and an evidence-led protection and recovery programme for Aberdeen Creek. The organization separates field evidence from proposals and technical hypotheses and does not claim municipal waste-collection authority.
