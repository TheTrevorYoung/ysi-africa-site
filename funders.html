What a program officer can diligence now

Capacity evidence, not just mission language.

YSI separates established controls from work still in progress so funders can see the real institutional position.

01Legal incorporation

Sierra Leone Company Limited by Guarantee; registration details and the current registry-name discrepancy are disclosed on the governance page.

02Field operating record

Documented cleanup, collection, waste handling, transport and material-separation experience from Lumley Beach.

03Financial controls

An interim financial-management framework establishes documentation, approvals, reconciliation and restricted-fund controls.

04Safeguarding & safety

An adopted working safeguarding policy and interim field health-and-safety standard are in force, with implementation mechanisms still being strengthened.

05Evidence discipline

Field work, organizational correspondence, development concepts and public quantitative claims are deliberately separated.

06Open readiness items

Current Board verification, institutional bank-account evidence and several implementation controls remain visible due-diligence priorities rather than hidden gaps.
