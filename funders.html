Current funding priorities

Two bounded opportunities — not an open-ended request for support.

YSI's current fundraising strategy does not seek funding for a full waste-processing facility or predetermined restoration programme. Current investment units are evidence, operating design and decision-making phases.

Current funding proposition

Waste Project — Phase 0: Evidence & Operational Design

Objective: determine where identifiable coastal waste leakage persists, what reaches marine and wetland environments, what can realistically be intercepted or recovered, where material should be routed, what it costs, and what additional infrastructure — if any — is actually justified.

Stage
Development / evidence-design stage. Physical deployment remains conditional on permissions, operating relationships and final methodology.
Duration
To be set in the approved Phase 0 technical design; YSI is not publishing an arbitrary duration before the scope is constrained.
Decision gate
Phase 0 ends with a GO / NO-GO / MODIFY recommendation on any later resource-recovery unit or infrastructure.
Expected Phase 0 outputs
  • defined pilot geography and leakage points;
  • measurement and characterization methodology;
  • quantity, composition and pathway evidence;
  • realistic recovery and routing options using legitimate existing infrastructure where practical;
  • interception, handling and routing cost evidence;
  • permissions, provider and operating requirements;
  • evidence-backed recommendation on what should be built next, if anything.
Funding requirement: a costed Phase 0 budget is not yet approved for public release. The amount will be derived from final scope, sites, methodology, permissions, provider requirements, staffing, equipment, transport, data needs, local quotations and contingency — not from a round-number fundraising target.
Current planning / funding-readiness stage

Aberdeen Creek — Baseline & Ecological Recovery Planning

Objective: establish a credible baseline, authority pathway and technical decision framework for future protection or ecological recovery work at Aberdeen Creek without predetermining planting, engineering or restoration actions.

Stage
Phase 0/1 evidence acquisition, authority coordination and technical review. Physical intervention remains behind project-specific gates.
Duration
To be finalized with the approved technical scope and authority pathway.
Decision gate
Proceed, modify, defer or no-go based on evidence, ecological suitability, authority requirements and technical review.
Potential first-phase outputs
  • authoritative boundary / GIS evidence;
  • georeferenced field baseline and repeatable observation points;
  • waste-leakage and stressor assessment;
  • community and legitimate resource-use baseline;
  • technical ecology / hydrology review preparation;
  • restoration or source-control options analysis;
  • monitoring framework and GO / MODIFY / NO-GO decision.
Funding requirement: no public project amount is presented until the first-phase scope, authority pathway and cost basis are sufficiently defined. YSI will not turn an indicative assumption into a public funding claim.

Partnership types

YSI is looking for capability as well as capital.

Partnership categories describe the kind of support YSI may need; they do not imply that any organization currently holds that role.

Funding partners

Support clearly bounded evidence, pilot, monitoring or institutional-development work with proportionate reporting requirements.

Technical partners

Waste characterization, sampling, circular-systems, ecology, hydrology, environmental safeguards and other specialist input where genuinely required.

Research & data partners

Measurement methods, GIS, monitoring, analysis, data quality, documentation and knowledge products.

Public-institution coordination

Authority, compliance, municipal-system alignment and access to relevant public information or infrastructure.

Responsible private-sector partners

Logistics, markets, recovery pathways, responsible sponsorship and implementation support where interests and environmental standards align.

Equipment / infrastructure

Considered only after evidence shows what equipment or infrastructure is technically justified; YSI is not seeking machinery simply because it may be available.

Forwardable materials

Designed for institutional review.

Program officers can download and circulate these materials internally. They are not substitutes for full due diligence.
