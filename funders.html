Current funding priorities

Two bounded opportunities.

YSI's internal field, evidence, routing, technical-review and final-audit architecture is built. Funding will be attached to bounded, costed field designs—not predetermined facilities or interventions.

Current funding proposition

Freetown Circular Waste Recovery Project — Phase 0

Purpose: determine where coastal waste leakage persists, what can realistically be intercepted or recovered, where material should be routed, what it costs and what should be built next—if anything.

Stage
Evidence and operational design. Physical deployment remains conditional on permissions, operating relationships and final methodology.
Funding produces
A defined pilot geography, auditable measurement and characterization, routing and cost evidence, and operating requirements.
Decision
GO / NO-GO / MODIFY recommendation on any later resource-recovery unit or infrastructure.
Core outputs
  • pilot geography and leakage-point definition;
  • quantity, composition and pathway evidence;
  • recovery, routing and cost analysis;
  • evidence-backed next-stage recommendation.
Costing: the Phase 0 requirement will be shared after scope, site, methodology, authority pathway and provider requirements are sufficiently defined and approved.
Planning / funding-readiness stage

Aberdeen Creek Protection & Recovery Project — Baseline

Purpose: establish a credible baseline, authority pathway and technical decision framework before selecting any planting, engineering or restoration action.

Stage
Evidence acquisition, authority coordination and technical review. Physical intervention remains behind project-specific gates.
Funding produces
Boundary and GIS evidence, georeferenced field baseline, stressor analysis, technical-review inputs and monitoring design.
Decision
Proceed, modify, defer or no-go based on evidence, ecological suitability, authority requirements and technical review.
Core outputs
  • authoritative boundary and georeferenced baseline;
  • waste-leakage and stressor assessment;
  • technical options and monitoring framework;
  • GO / MODIFY / NO-GO recommendation.
Costing: the first-phase requirement will follow the approved technical scope, authority pathway and current local cost evidence.
