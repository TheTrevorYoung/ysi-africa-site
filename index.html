Why YSI exists

Cleanup showed us where the system breaks.

Waste on a beach is the visible end of a larger chain that runs through homes, businesses, streets, drains, transport and disposal.

YSI works backward from that visible problem. Field access helps us identify leakage, measure material, test recovery routes, understand costs and determine what is actually missing.

The operating logic

Intercept → Measure → Sort → Recover → Route → Learn → Replicate.

YSI does not need to own every part of the waste system. The goal is to identify leakage, use legitimate existing routes where they work, and build only what evidence shows is missing.

01InterceptTest lawful ways to capture identifiable leakage before it reaches sensitive environments.
02MeasureRecord weight, location, time and method.
03SortCharacterize recoverables, hazards and residuals.
04RecoverAssess realistic reuse, recycling and other supported pathways.
05RouteUse legitimate existing infrastructure and approved providers where practical.
06LearnMeasure costs, bottlenecks, controls and actual gaps.
07ReplicateScale only after a model proves repeatable, compliant and useful.
