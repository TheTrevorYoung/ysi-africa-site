Waste Project · Current stage: Phase 0

Measure first.
Build second.

YSI is developing a coastal waste-leakage and resource-recovery initiative for Freetown. The current stage is evidence and operational design: find where leakage persists, measure and characterize it, test realistic recovery and routing options, understand real costs and decide what additional infrastructure — if any — is justified.

Phase 0 is non-thermal. RDF/SRF and other thermal pathways remain later technical hypotheses, not the current fundraising proposition.

About YSI

Independent, community-driven and nonpartisan.

YSI Africa is the public brand of Young Sustainability Initiative, a legally incorporated Sierra Leonean environmental nonprofit organized as a Company Limited by Guarantee.

Its constitutional objects support environmental initiatives including waste management, beach cleanup, pollution reduction, sustainability, biodiversity and ecosystem protection, community education, outreach and public accountability.

YSI's institutional direction is to prove practical systems locally and replicate only what evidence supports.

Transparency & accountability

Credibility comes before scale.

YSI separates verified facts, documented activity, current development, technical hypotheses and institutional engagement so the public record does not outrun the evidence.
