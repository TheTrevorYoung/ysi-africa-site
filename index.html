Freetown Circular Waste Recovery Project · Current stage: Phase 0

Measure first.
Build second.

Phase 0 is designed to identify where leakage persists, measure and characterize it, test realistic recovery and routing options, understand real costs and decide what additional infrastructure — if any — is justified.

The purpose of Phase 0 is a decision — not a predetermined facility.

What YSI focuses on

Two core systems. One cross-cutting participation layer.

YSI's near-term operating concentration is waste and resource recovery plus coastal and wetland protection, supported by community participation and environmental learning.

♻

Waste & Resource Recovery

Cleanup, measurement, characterization, sorting, recovery and routing systems developed only as evidence supports them.

≈

Coastal & Wetland Protection

Evidence-led protection and recovery planning for beaches, waterways, wetlands and coastal ecosystems.

◎

Community Participation & Environmental Learning

Practical participation, field information and environmental learning tied to real project work rather than stand-alone awareness activity.
