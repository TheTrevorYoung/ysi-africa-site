Why YSI exists

Cleanup showed us where the system breaks.

Waste on a beach is the visible end of a much larger system. It has moved through homes, businesses, streets, drains, transport routes and disposal pathways before reaching the coast.

YSI's direction is to work backward from that visible problem. We use cleanup and field access to understand waste streams, test interception, measure material, identify legitimate recovery routes and determine what infrastructure is actually missing.

The operating logic

Intercept → Measure → Sort → Recover → Route → Learn → Replicate.

The goal is not to own every part of the waste system. It is to identify leakage, work inside legitimate existing arrangements and build only what evidence shows is missing.

01InterceptTest lawful ways to capture identifiable leakage before it reaches sensitive environments.
02MeasureWeigh and record material by location, time and method.
03SortCharacterize the stream and separate recoverables, hazards and residuals.
04RecoverAssess realistic reuse, recycling or other supported recovery pathways.
05RouteUse legitimate existing infrastructure and approved providers where practical.
06LearnMeasure costs, bottlenecks, controls and actual infrastructure gaps.
07ReplicateScale only after a model proves repeatable, compliant and useful.
