YSI Africa flagship development concept

Local Waste.
Local Value.
Local Energy.

YSI is developing a modular waste-interception and circular resource-recovery system for Freetown: capture waste closer to where it is produced, measure it, recover useful materials, and turn suitable residual material into process energy.

This flagship sits within YSI's wider environmental work in sanitation, recycling, coastal and wetland protection, community education and practical sustainability systems.

About YSI

Independent, community-driven and nonpartisan.

YSI Africa is the public brand of Young Sustainability Initiative, a legally incorporated Sierra Leonean environmental nonprofit organized as a Company Limited by Guarantee.

Its constitutional objects expressly support environmental initiatives including waste management, beach cleanup, pollution reduction, sustainability, biodiversity and ecosystem protection, community education, outreach and public accountability.

Our approach is simple: identify practical environmental problems, build credible solutions, document the work and develop the relationships needed to sustain impact.

Transparency & accountability

Credibility comes before scale.

YSI will not claim staff, partners, projects, legal designations or impact numbers that cannot be supported.
