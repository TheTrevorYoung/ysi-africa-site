## Replacement protocol

As YSI commissions new photography in Sierra Leone, replace public context images with YSI owned images that match the same visual purpose: bright daylight, clear environmental setting, visible human or ecological context where appropriate, and strong geographic relevance. Preserve the evidence distinction between documentary project evidence and illustrative context.
