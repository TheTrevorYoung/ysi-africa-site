Why YSI exists

Field action is the beginning—not the final product.

Environmental problems such as unmanaged waste, coastal pollution and wetland degradation are not solved by isolated activities alone. YSI is being built to understand the operating problem, test practical responses, document what happens and develop systems that can improve over time.

Cleanup gives YSI direct access to real waste streams, field conditions, community realities and operating constraints. That experience becomes a foundation for measurement, recovery testing, technical validation and stronger environmental infrastructure.

The long-term objective is not to run occasional campaigns. It is to build practical environmental systems that can be proven locally and replicated when the evidence supports expansion.

What we focus on

Four connected environmental priorities.

YSI concentrates on areas where field action, environmental protection and circular systems can reinforce each other.

01

Waste Management & Sanitation

Cleaner public spaces, localized waste interception, sanitation, measurement and practical operating systems.

02

Recycling & Circular Economy

Sorting, material recovery, reuse, waste-to-value and responsible resource-recovery pathways.

03

Coastal & Wetland Protection

Reducing waste leakage, protecting coastal environments and developing evidence-led approaches to wetland recovery.

04

Community Education & Stewardship

Environmental awareness and participation connected to practical action, local knowledge and long-term stewardship.

How YSI develops systems

Move from activity to evidence to scale.

YSI's development model deliberately slows down before scale. A project should earn the right to expand through evidence, technical validation and institutional fit.

01
Field action & accessWork directly with real environmental conditions and real waste streams.
02
Measure & characterizeTurn experience into data, documented observations and clearer problem definition.
03
Recover & test valueSeparate materials and test realistic recovery, reuse and waste-to-value pathways.
04
Validate technically & institutionallyTest whether the approach is environmentally sound, compliant, operationally workable and economically credible.
05
Replicate only what worksScale proven systems—not assumptions.

Institutional discipline

Credibility comes before scale.

YSI is young as an institution, so credibility has to be built deliberately. That means being precise about evidence, relationships, project status and authority.

01Evidence before claimsYSI's historical 20,000+ kg public impact claim is supported by documented field activity; the original mass-calculation record is under reconstruction.
02Real relationships onlyTargets, prospects and technical contacts are not automatically described as partners or funders.
03Clear project statusCompleted field work, development-stage projects and technical hypotheses are labeled separately.
04Compliance before interventionPhysical environmental work should proceed only through the applicable authority and technical gates.
