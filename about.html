About YSI Africa

Young Sustainability Initiative — practical environmental systems for Sierra Leone.

YSI Africa is the public brand of Young Sustainability Initiative, a legally incorporated Sierra Leonean environmental nonprofit organized as a Company Limited by Guarantee.

Who we are

Community-driven. Independent. Systems-focused.

YSI works to build practical, scalable environmental systems in Sierra Leone. Our initial operational concentration is Freetown.

Our work connects direct field action with stronger measurement, evidence, technical development, institutional relationships and long-term operating models.

YSI does not present development-stage concepts as completed projects or prospective institutions as partners.

Focus areas

Environmental problems that require practical systems.

Waste management & sanitation

Localized waste interception, sanitation, cleaner public spaces, measurement and stronger operating systems.

Recycling & circular economy

Sorting, material recovery, reuse, waste-to-value and responsible resource-recovery pathways.

Coastal & wetland protection

Supporting cleaner beaches, reducing waste leakage and advocating for responsible protection of coastal ecosystems and wetlands.

Community education

Environmental awareness, participation and local stewardship tied to practical environmental action.

How YSI develops systems

Cleanup is the starting point, not the final product.

Cleanup → Measurement → Characterization → Sorting → Material Recovery → Value Creation → Technical Validation → Commercial / Institutional Validation → Scalable Operating Model

Field activity gives YSI access to real waste streams, operating conditions and evidence. The objective is to use that evidence to test better systems and replicate only what works.
